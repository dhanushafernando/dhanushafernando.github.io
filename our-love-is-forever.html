Director's Statement

As a filmmaker, I am deeply interested in exploring human emotions, relationships and the psychological experiences that shape our lives.

Through Our Love Is Forever, I wanted to explore the emotional consequences of losing a loved one and the powerful connection between a parent and a child. The film follows a family struggling with the death of their only son, while the child's spirit remains connected to his mother, creating an emotional journey shaped by love, grief and tragedy.

I wanted to explore how people experience loss differently and how unbearable emotional pain can affect the human mind. The story also reflects the importance of understanding the people we love, especially when they are going through experiences that we cannot see or fully understand.

My intention was not simply to tell a supernatural tragedy, but to present an emotional story about family bonds, unconditional love, grief and the fragile nature of human relationships.

With Our Love Is Forever, I hope audiences will look beyond what is visible, recognise the depth of human emotions and reflect on the importance of love, understanding and compassion.
