Synopsis

Since childhood, Kasun has dreamed of becoming a Ves dancer, but despite years of determination and sacrifice, his dream remains unfulfilled. After his mother’s emergency surgery consumes his savings, he works as a cross-dresser at a nightclub to earn money for his Ves Mangalyaya. However, he falls seriously ill and is diagnosed with a condition known as Night Blinder. Following treatment, he returns home, only to face humiliation, verbal abuse and physical violence when his mother discovers him wearing women’s clothing. Overwhelmed by rejection, emotional distress and despair, Kasun reaches a breaking point and contemplates ending his life. In his final moments, reality gives way to imagination, and he envisions himself adorned in full Ves attire, dancing gracefully to the rhythm of traditional drums. Though he never fulfils his childhood dream in reality, he experiences it through vivid mental images, leaving a poignant reflection on unfulfilled dreams, the cruelty of fate and the devastating consequences of social prejudice and rejection.

Director's Statement

As a filmmaker, I believe that every human being carries a dream, but not everyone gets the opportunity to fulfil it. Sometimes, the greatest obstacles do not come from fate alone, but from the society and people from whom we expect love and understanding.

Through Nettuwek, I wanted to explore the emotional struggles of a person whose childhood dream becomes a painful journey of sacrifice, rejection and unfulfilled hopes. Kasun's story represents more than an individual struggle. It reflects the experiences of people who are misunderstood, judged and rejected simply because they do not fit into society's expectations.

The traditional art of Ves dancing is an important part of Sri Lankan cultural identity. I wanted to bring this cultural element into a story that also explores personal identity, dreams and the human need for acceptance.

My intention was not simply to tell a tragic story, but to encourage audiences to look beyond appearances, question social prejudice and understand the emotional pain that can exist behind someone's silence.

With Nettuwek, I hope viewers will realise that a little understanding, acceptance and kindness can make a difference in another person's life.
