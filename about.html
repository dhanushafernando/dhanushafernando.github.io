About Me

Dhanusha Fernando is an award-winning Sri Lankan film director, writer and editor with a passion for cinematic storytelling and exploring the emotional and psychological dimensions of human experiences.

With a strong interest in every aspect of filmmaking, including directing, screenwriting, cinematography, editing and visual effects, he believes in creating meaningful cinematic experiences that connect with audiences.

His first short film, Our Love Is Forever, has received international recognition, earning 56 awards at film festivals. His achievements include winning the Best Director Award in Kolkata, India, in 2023, and the Best Director Award at the Black Cat Award International Film Festival in Bolivia.

As a filmmaker, Dhanusha is particularly interested in exploring human emotions, social realities, psychological struggles and untold stories through a distinctive cinematic approach.

Beyond directing and editing, his creative interests extend to photography, visual arts, makeup and visual effects. He believes that understanding different creative departments helps a filmmaker develop a stronger artistic vision.

Through his films, he aims to create thought-provoking stories that leave a lasting impression on audiences beyond the screen.

"Cinema is not only about telling stories; it is about making people feel, think and remember."
