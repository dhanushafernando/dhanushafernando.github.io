Selected Works

01 / SHORT FILM

Our Love Is Forever

AWARD-WINNING DEBUT SHORT FILM

A deeply emotional story exploring love, loss, family and the psychological consequences of an unexpected tragedy.

A child who does not know that he has died continues trying to live with his parents. While his parents struggle with the loss of their only son, the story unfolds through a tragic journey involving love, grief and the supernatural.

Recognition: 56 international film festival awards.

Role: Director, Writer and Editor.

02 / SHORT FILM

A Dancer (නැට්ටුවෙක්)

PSYCHOLOGICAL DRAMA

A story about dreams, sacrifice, identity and the struggles of an individual pursuing a dream against the difficult realities of life.

Kasun dreams of becoming a Ves dancer. When his mother needs emergency surgery, his savings are used for her treatment. To earn money, he takes unexpected work at a nightclub, facing personal struggles, rejection and difficult circumstances.

Original Language: Sinhala.

Role: Director, Story, Screenplay, Editor and VFX.
