AWARDS & SPECIAL RECOGNITION

VISUAL ARTIST OF THE YEAR – SRI LANKA 2025

Honoured to be the recipient of the New Generation – 6th Youth Top 40 Award 2025 in the category of Visual Artist of the Year.

Although I was unable to attend the award ceremony due to health reasons, my award was graciously received on stage on my behalf by Kamila Medis Sir.

My heartfelt gratitude goes to Shehan Viduranga, Kamila Medis Sir, Dr. Sulochana Segera, Women in Management – Sri Lanka, New Generation Sri Lanka and SOORYA for this wonderful recognition and honour.
