About Me

Dhanusha Fernando is an award-winning Sri Lankan film director, writer and editor with a passion for cinematic storytelling and exploring the emotional and psychological dimensions of human experiences. With interests in directing, screenwriting, cinematography, editing, visual effects and makeup, he believes in creating meaningful cinematic experiences that connect with audiences. His creative journey is driven by a passion for human emotions, social realities, psychological struggles and untold stories.
