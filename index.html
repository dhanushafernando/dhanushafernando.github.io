About Me

Dhanusha Fernando is an award-winning Sri Lankan film director, writer and editor passionate about cinematic storytelling and exploring the emotional and psychological dimensions of human experiences.

His first short film, Our Love Is Forever, has received international recognition, earning 56 awards at film festivals. His creative journey also includes recognition in directing, cinematography, prosthetic makeup and visual arts.

Through his films, Dhanusha aims to explore human emotions, social realities, psychological struggles and untold stories through a distinctive cinematic approach.
